# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should remain respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Include relevant links or screenshots while removing unrelated personal information.
